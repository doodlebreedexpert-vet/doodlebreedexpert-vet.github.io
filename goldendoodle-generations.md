# Goldendoodle Generations, Simply Explained

People ask me this all the time: what do F1, F1B and F2 actually mean, and does it matter? It does. Mostly for the coat.

**F1** is a straight cross, Golden Retriever x Poodle. You get hybrid vigor, but the coat is a gamble. Some shed a fair bit.

**F1B** is an F1 bred back to a Poodle. It's about 75% Poodle, so the coat is usually curlier and sheds less. It's often the pick for families with mild allergies.

**F2 and up** are doodle x doodle crosses. Litters can be less predictable unless the breeder has worked the lines for several generations.
