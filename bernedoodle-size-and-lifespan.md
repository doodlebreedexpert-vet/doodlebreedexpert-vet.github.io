# Bernedoodle Size and Lifespan: What to Expect

Bernese Mountain Dogs are lovely, but they're known for short lives, often 7 to 8 years. Crossing with a Poodle helps. Most Bernedoodles live noticeably longer, and smaller ones tend to live longest.

**Standard Bernedoodles** can top 70 lb and usually live around 12 years.

**Mini Bernedoodles** come in around 25 to 50 lb. They fit apartments better and often reach their mid-teens. Cost, size charts and temperament are in the [Mini Bernedoodle breed guide](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/mini-bernedoodle/).

Lifespan isn't only about size. Parent health testing (hips, elbows, eyes and the heart) makes a big difference.
