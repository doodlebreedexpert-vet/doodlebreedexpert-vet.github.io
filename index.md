# Doodle Breed Guide

Doodles aren't one breed. They're a family of Poodle crosses, and they can be very different from each other. A Bernedoodle and an Aussiedoodle may both be called "doodles", but one is a laid-back gentle giant and the other needs a job every day. This page is a quick starting point. Each section links to the full, vet-reviewed guide on [Doodle Breed Expert](https://doodlebreedexpert.com).

## Goldendoodle

Golden Retriever x Poodle. Friendly with almost everyone, easy to train, and comes in sizes from toy to standard. The coat needs regular grooming. Full details in the [Goldendoodle breed guide](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/goldendoodle-breed-guide/).

## Bernedoodle

Bernese Mountain Dog x Poodle. Calm and affectionate, sometimes a bit stubborn. The tri-color coats are striking. Compare breeders by region on the [Bernedoodle breeders page](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/category/breeders/bernedoodle/).

## Aussiedoodle

Australian Shepherd x Poodle. Very smart and high energy, and it does best with an active family. Merle coloring is common. See the [Aussiedoodle breeders directory](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/category/breeders/aussiedoodle/).

## Australian Labradoodle

Multigenerational lines bred for steady temperament and more predictable coats. Find breeders on the [Australian Labradoodle page](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/category/breeders/australian-labradoodle/).

## Choosing the right doodle

Think about your energy level, your space and your grooming budget before you think about looks. Generation (F1, F1B, multigen) affects coat and shedding more than most buyers expect. Browse every breed at [doodlebreedexpert.com](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/category/doodle-breed/).
