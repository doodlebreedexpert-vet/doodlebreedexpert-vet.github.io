# Aussiedoodle Colors and What They Mean for Health

Aussiedoodles come in more colors than almost any other doodle, thanks to the Australian Shepherd side. Some colors are just looks. Others need a closer look at the parents.

**Blue merle and red merle** are the showstoppers. Merle is fine on its own. The problem is breeding two merles together, which raises the risk of deafness and eye defects in puppies. Always ask whether both parents are merle. More on this in the [blue merle Aussiedoodle guide](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/blue-merle-aussiedoodle/) and the [red merle Aussiedoodle guide](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/red-merle-aussiedoodle/).

**Black** and **chocolate** are solid colors with no merle-related risk. They're often a bit cheaper too. See the [black Aussiedoodle](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/black-aussiedoodle/) and [chocolate Aussiedoodle](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/chocolate-aussiedoodle/) pages.

Whatever color you want, the breeder matters more than the coat. This [checklist for vetting an Aussiedoodle breeder](https://doodlebreedexpert.com/how-to-find-a-reputable-aussiedoodle-breeder/) covers the red flags I'd watch for.
